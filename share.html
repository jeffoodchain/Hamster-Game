📖 How to Play

Care for your hamster

  • 🌻 Drag a seed from the basket onto your hamster to feed.
  • 💧 Click the water bottle to drink.
  • 🏡 Click the hut to nap (restores energy).
  • 🛁 Click the sand bath to clean off.
  • ♥ Click your hamster to pet — boosts happiness.

Earn coins

  • ♻ Click any poop or shell to bin it. (+1💰)
  • 🎡 Wheel runs (+3💰), 🪵 chew log (+1💰), 🪜 ladder slide (+2💰)
  • 🌧️ Treat Rain mini-game on a 90s cooldown — catch falling treats.
  • 🌟 Daily login streak — coming back grows your bonus.

Shop & customization

  • 🏪 Buy toys, treats, costumes, and habitat themes.
  • 🎀 Costumes appear on your hamster's head.
  • 🌿 Themes repaint the cage walls and floor.
  • 🥕 Treats give big stat boosts when fed.

Other things to notice

  • 👋 A friend hamster occasionally visits the cage.
  • ☀️🌙 The day/night cycle changes the lighting and music.
  • 🦋 Butterflies appear by day; fireflies at night.
  • 🐹 Your hamster looks visibly tired/dirty when stats drop.
  • 🏆 Achievements track milestones — view them with the trophy button.
